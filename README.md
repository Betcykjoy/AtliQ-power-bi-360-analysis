# AtliQ-power-bi-360-analysis
This integrated dashboard delivers a holistic view of key business performance across Finance, Sales, Supply Chain, and Marketing. By highlighting profitability, growth trends, forecasting accuracy, and risk exposure, it equips executives with actionable insights to support informed, data-driven decisions and strategic planning

Key Features and Dashboard Views
Home Page: Provides a centralized navigation hub for accessing different business functions through an intuitive interface.
Finance View: Presents detailed Profit and Loss (P&L) analysis across customers, products, and regions, with flexible time-based comparisons.
Sales View: Evaluates customer performance using Net Sales (NS), Gross Margin (GM), and Year-to-Date (YTD) and Year-to-Go (YTG) metrics.
Marketing View: Analyzes product and market performance with interactive comparisons of Gross Margin % and Net Margin %.
Supply Chain View: Monitors forecast accuracy, demand variability, and risk profiles to support inventory planning and supply chain optimization.
Executive View: Summarizes critical business KPIs and performance trends to support high-level strategic planning.
Support Section: Provides a dedicated channel for users to access assistance and resolve dashboard-related issues.
